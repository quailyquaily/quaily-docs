# Subscriptions

In [Subscribers](https://quaily.com/dashboard/subscribers), you can manage your channel members.

## What You Can Do

- View your subscriber list
- Filter by email or membership type
- Update a member's subscription status
- Remove subscribers

## Add Subscribers Manually (Credits)

You can add subscribers from the dashboard by email addresses.

1. Open **Subscribers**.
2. Click **Add Subscribers**.
3. Enter one email address per line.
4. Confirm and save.

Credits are charged for this feature:

- Cost: 20 Credits per subscriber
- Charge model: pay-as-you-go, based on the number of members added in the request
- If your Credits are insufficient, top up first in [Profile - Credits](https://quaily.com/dashboard/profile/credits)
