# Monetization Settings

In [Monetization Settings](https://quaily.com/dashboard/lists/@current/settings/payment), you can configure how your channel earns money.

## What Is Included

- Payment settings (fiat / crypto / self-hosted payment)
- Payout settings
- Ads settings
- Credits-related feature access

## Credits and Monetization

Some monetization features depend on your Credits balance:

- Enabling self-hosted payment requires sufficient Credits
- Self-hosted payment service fee is deducted from Credits per completed order
- Extra Sending Quota, Add Subscribers Manually, and Import posts from Internet are billed in Credits

Manage Credits in [Profile - Credits](https://quaily.com/dashboard/profile/credits).
